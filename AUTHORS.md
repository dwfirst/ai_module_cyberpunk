## Development history

### Stage 1 — Initial brainstorming (Gemini)

The core idea was developed by the author in a brainstorming session with **Google Gemini**.
This session produced the first direction, the luxury positioning, and the general feature list.

### Stage 2 — Visual concepts (Gemini, Nano Banana)

The four visual renders in `assets/renders/` were generated with **Gemini's Nano Banana** image model.
They show the dock, the open rack, the bench prototype, and the server-room variant.

### Stage 3 — Engineering concept (DeepSeek)

The author then developed the full written concept in collaboration with **DeepSeek**.
This stage produced:

- the brand-focused README,
- the swarm model and security module notes,
- the honest limits and open questions,
- the repository structure and documentation.

The collaboration had a rough patch: the assistant lost track of context and mixed up repository paths. The author caught the error, redirected the work, and the concept was completed under his guidance.

All engineering decisions, scope cuts, and final wording were made by the author.
The AI assistant served as a structured brainstorming partner, critic, and technical editor.
